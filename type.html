Types of Mental Health Problems

Mood Disorders

  • Seasonal affective disorder – depression linked to seasonal changes
  • Persistent depressive disorder – long-lasting mild depression

Anxiety-Related Disorders

  • Panic disorder – sudden intense panic attacks
  • Social anxiety disorder – fear of social situations
  • Generalized anxiety disorder – constant excessive worry
  • Phobia – intense fear of specific objects or situations

Trauma and Stress Disorders

  • Acute stress disorder – short-term reaction after trauma
  • Adjustment disorder – difficulty coping with major life changes
Personality Disorders
  • Borderline personality disorder – unstable emotions and relationships
  • Narcissistic personality disorder – excessive need for admiration
  • Antisocial personality disorder – disregard for others’ rights
Psychotic Disorders
  • Schizoaffective disorder – symptoms of schizophrenia and mood disorders together

Eating Disorders

  • Anorexia nervosa – extreme restriction of food intake
  • Bulimia nervosa – binge eating followed by purging

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

  • Learning disability – difficulty with learning skills
  • Tourette syndrome – repeated involuntary movements or sounds

Sleep and Behavior Disorders

  • Insomnia – difficulty sleeping
  • Conduct disorder – aggressive or rule-breaking behavior in children

Substance-Related Disorders

  • Alcohol use disorder
  • Drug addiction
